Cookie Policy

Last updated: March 2026

What are cookies

Cookies are small files stored in your browser. Glimpsy uses browser local storage (similar to cookies) to remember your preferences. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

What we store

NamePurposeDurationType
glimpsy_cookie_consent Remembers your cookie preference so we don't show the banner on every visit Until you clear browser storage Essential
glimpsy_admin Keeps admin users logged in (admin area only) 8 hours Essential
Child profile data Stores the child profiles you create (name, age, content preferences) locally in your browser Until you clear browser storage Functional

Third-party cookies

Google Fonts (used to load our typefaces) may set cookies or log requests. We do not control these. No advertising networks or analytics platforms are used on Glimpsy.

Managing cookies

You can clear all locally stored data by clearing your browser's site data for glimpsy.uk. This will remove your child profiles and consent preference.

Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies via their settings. Note that blocking essential storage may affect how Glimpsy works.
